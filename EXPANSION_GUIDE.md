## Overview
The application has been massively expanded with 9 new comprehensive analytics tabs, transforming it into a full-featured inventory intelligence platform.

---

## 📋 Tab 1: Worksheet Mode (Original)
**Your existing functionality**
- Power Parts, All Parts, MTD, Bunnings, Spring Stock Forecast workflows
- Unchanged - all original features intact

---

## 📊 Tab 2: Analytics Dashboard
**Real-time inventory health and KPIs**
- **Key Metrics**: Total items, inventory value, average WOH, critical items, forecast accuracy
- **Inventory Health**: Status breakdown pie chart, WOH distribution histogram
- **Forecast Trends**: Monthly trends visualization for top 10 items
- **Distribution**: SKU distribution by category
- **Aging Analysis**: Inventory aging brackets
- **Top Movers**: High-demand items identification

---

## ⚠️ Tab 3: Risk Analysis & Alerts
**Identify supply chain vulnerabilities**
- **Risk Scoring**: Automated risk score calculation (0-100)
- **Risk Levels**: CRITICAL → HIGH → MEDIUM → LOW
- **Risk Drivers Analysis**:
  - Low Weeks on Hand
  - High Forecast Demand
  - Urgent Status Items
  - Pending Orders
- **Critical Alerts**: Exportable list of items requiring immediate action
- **Mitigation Strategies**: Automated recommendations for each risk category
- **Dashboard**: Risk matrix visualization, risk distribution charts

---

## 🔮 Tab 4: Predictive Insights & AI
**Machine learning-powered forecasting**
- **Growth Forecast**: 3-month vs 12-month growth comparison
  - 🚀 Accelerating (>50% growth)
  - 📈 Growing (20-50%)
  - ➡️ Stable (-20% to +20%)
  - 📉 Declining (-20% to -50%)
  - 🔻 Collapsing (<-50%)
- **Demand Elasticity**: Volatility analysis, demand pattern recognition
- **Optimal Stock Levels**: Dynamic recommendations based on forecast and usage
- **Anomaly Detection**: 
  - Extreme WOH values
  - Sudden demand spikes
  - Items with zero recent demand
- **AI Recommendations**: Automated insights for safety stock, promotions, expediting

---

## 📈 Tab 5: Trend Analysis
**Historical trends and seasonal patterns**
- **Monthly Trends**: 6-month visualization of top items
- **Seasonal Patterns**: 
  - Seasonality index (+/- % from average)
  - Identifies peak and low periods
- **Category Trends**: Compare trends across product categories
- **Location Trends**: Performance by warehouse/location
- **Volatility Analysis**:
  - Coefficient of Variation (CV) classification
  - Stable, Moderate, High, Extreme volatility buckets
  - High volatility items flagged for safety stock adjustment

---

## ⚙️ Tab 6: Inventory Optimization
**Dynamic stock level recommendations**
- **Current vs Optimal Stock**: Visual comparison, variance analysis
- **Replenishment Strategies**:
  - Urgent (< 1 week lead time response)
  - Regular (1-2 weeks)
  - Bulk (monthly)
  - Automatic reorder quantity calculation
- **Safety Stock Calculator**:
  - Configurable service level targets (80-99%)
  - Lead time and demand variability inputs
  - Z-score based calculations
- **ABC Analysis (Pareto)**:
  - Class A items: 80% of value (tight control)
  - Class B items: 15% of value (medium control)
  - Class C items: 5% of value (standard control)
- **Cost Impact**:
  - Annual carrying costs
  - Annual ordering costs
  - Total inventory cost calculation
  - Cost optimization opportunities
